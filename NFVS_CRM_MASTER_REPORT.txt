SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PLATFORM GENESIS
====================================================================================================

1.1 Platform Genesis and Business Mission
The NFVS CRM platform was conceived and engineered to resolve a complex operational challenge:
simultaneously orchestrating the end-to-end administration, workforce management, financial accounting,
inventory control, legal compliance, and customer relationship pipelines of two structurally distinct yet
synergistic corporate entities under a unified technological umbrella:
  1. Naree Foundation: A premier non-profit social development organization focused on social welfare,
     community upliftment, women empowerment, donor relations, and field social initiatives.
  2. NF Venture Studio: A high-velocity commercial incubator, venture creation engine, and technological
     innovation hub developing proprietary digital products, managing startup incubation, and delivering commercial software.

1.2 High-Level Architectural Guarantees
- Absolute Tenant Segregation: Despite sharing a single codebase and relational database schema, data for
  Naree Foundation and NF Venture Studio is strictly segregated via tenant guards at the API and database levels.
- Flexible Resource Mobility: Cross-company workforce sharing is enabled via governed "On-Duty Assignments"
  and cross-company resource borrowing mechanisms without compromising entity-level security.
- Multi-Executive Visibility: Dedicated executive dashboards for Board Chairperson, CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, CMO, and HR Head.
- Complete Compliance: Dual-tax compliance supporting Indian GST, TDS, EPF, ESIC, professional tax deductions, and statutory audit logging.

SECTION 3: MULTI-TENANT ISOLATION & DUAL-COMPANY OPERATING MODEL
====================================================================================================

3.1 Tenant Isolation Architecture
Multi-tenancy in NFVS CRM is enforced via explicit foreign key relationships referencing the Organization model.
The session management engine stores the user active company context in a dedicated, tamper-proof cookie:
  Cookie Names: "nfvs_session" (User credentials, role, expiration) and "nfvs_active_company" (Selected Organization ID).

3.2 Scope Guard & Tenant Protection Logic
Every mutating API endpoint and sensitive data query executes through scope-guard.ts:
  - If user.roleCode === "SUPER_ADMIN" or user.dataScope === "GLOBAL", cross-tenant read/write is allowed.
  - For all other roles, activeCompany.id must match the resource organizationId or TenantIsolationError (HTTP 403) is raised.
  - Cross-company resource lending is governed via OnDutyAssignment records, granting temporary explicit access.

SECTION 4: ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) & PERMISSIONS SPECIFICATION
====================================================================================================

4.1 System Roles Hierarchy (14 Roles):
  1. SUPER_ADMIN (Level 100): Full unrestricted platform access across all companies, databases, and system configurations.
  2. ADMIN (Level 90): Company-level master administrator; manages user accounts, master settings, and organizational structure.
  3. CHAIRPERSON (Level 85): Executive oversight across all companies; high-level reports, financial summaries, and governance.
  4. CEO (Level 80): Chief Executive Officer; comprehensive operational, financial, and strategic decision-making authority.
  5. CTO (Level 75): Chief Technology Officer; IT operations, technical task assignment, sprint velocity, technical procurement.
  6. CIO (Level 75): Chief Information Officer; enterprise data governance, IT compliance, security policies.
  7. CMO (Level 75): Chief Marketing Officer; CRM campaigns, customer acquisition funnels, leads, marketing budgets.
  8. CFO (Level 75): Chief Financial Officer; general ledger, accounts, payroll authorization, banking, tax filings.
  9. COO (Level 75): Chief Operating Officer; procurement, warehouses, field operations, logistics, vendor contracts.
  10. HR (Level 60): Human Resources; employee lifecycle, recruitment, onboarding, leave approvals, attendance, payroll processing.
  11. DEPARTMENT_HEAD (Level 50): Departmental resource management, leave reviews, budget approvals, team workflows.
  12. MANAGER (Level 40): Operational delivery, milestone tracking, resource allocations, performance appraisals.
  13. TEAM_LEAD (Level 30): Daily task assignments, sprint tracking, peer reviews, initial leave endorsements.
  14. EMPLOYEE (Level 10): Self-service portal: payslip access, leave submission, attendance clocking, personal tasks.

4.2 Data Scopes Matrix:
  - SELF: User can only access records created by or directly assigned to their employee profile.
  - TEAM: User can access records belonging to members of their operational team.
  - DEPARTMENT: User can access all records within their assigned department.
  - BUSINESS_UNIT: User can access all records within a dedicated business unit.
  - COMPANY: User can access all records belonging to their active corporate organization.
  - GLOBAL: Cross-tenant visibility across both Naree Foundation and NF Venture Studio.

SECTION 9: CROSS-COMPANY RESOURCE LENDING, WORKFLOWS & AUTOMATIONS
====================================================================================================

9.1 Cross-Company Resource Lending (Borrowing Protocol)
To maximize talent utility across Naree Foundation and NF Venture Studio, the system implements
a formalized Cross-Company Resource Lending engine:
  1. Requisition: A project manager or department head in Company A submits a borrowing request for a specialist in Company B.
  2. Authorization: The lending company Department Head and HR approve the requisition.
  3. OnDutyAssignment Record: An OnDutyAssignment record is established with start date, end date, and billable cost center.
  4. Dynamic Context: When the borrowed employee clocks in or logs hours under Company A, their attendance
     and work output are cross-referenced without transferring their master employee contract.

9.2 Automated Escalation Engine
The escalation engine monitors critical approvals (leave requests, purchase orders, expenses, and task blockers):
  - Level 1 Escalation: If a Manager does not approve a request within 48 hours, an automated alert is issued.
  - Level 2 Escalation: If unresolved after 72 hours, the request automatically escalates to the Department Head / Director.
  - Level 3 Escalation: Executive visibility dashboard flags blocked operations to the COO / CEO.
